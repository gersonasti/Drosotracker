We track the life cycle, not behaviour.
Tracks the Drosophila melanogaster life cycle, tasks and lifespan. Temperature-dependent development model (thermal summation, threshold ≈ 11.8 °C · ~116 degree-days, calibrated for ~15–28 °C). Estimated times · Data stored locally in your browser. Privacy policy

Calibrate a genotype

Fine-tune the development model for a specific genotype using a real observation.
Keep the egg-lay window short (1–3 h): remove the parents afterwards so all eggs are ~the same age. That short window is what gives a good calibration.

Before you start: where your data lives

Rename this vial

The name is just what you see; the logged counts stay attached to the vial no matter what it is called. Leave it empty to go back to the default.

Leave this vial out

Decide this on something you saw in the vial, not on how the curve looks. It is written down with the day and reported alongside the result.
